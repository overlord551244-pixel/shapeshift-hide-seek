How to play

  • Hiders get a short prep time to shapeshift into any person or animal on the map, and pick a color. Then you're stuck with it.
  • The map is full of NPCs that look just like you. Walk, stop, and blend in.
  • The seeker has a gun. Shooting a hider catches them, but shooting an NPC costs a heart.
  • Hiders win if time runs out or the seeker loses all their hearts. The seeker wins by catching everyone.
  • Everyone can use items. Check them out during prep!
